Plumbers

People call a plumber when something is already leaking. They pick from whoever shows up first with the right address and a phone number that works.

What usually goes wrong, from reviews of real Permian Basin sites

  • The site gives Google no structured address, phone or hours, so it may show old information.
  • The title and description name only Midland, so someone in Odessa searching for a plumber sees a different city.
  • Links shared on Facebook or in a text show no picture.

What a review usually finds.

Typical problems from reviews of local HVAC websites in the Permian Basin. Business names removed, the problems are real.

Google writes your search snippet for you.

The home page has no description, so Google picks a line from the page. It may not be the one that makes someone call.

Search engines can't read your address and hours.

The site has no structured business details, so your address, phone and hours aren't handed to Google in a form it reads directly.

Shared links show a blank box.

Paste your site into Facebook or a text message and no preview picture appears, which makes the link easy to scroll past.

The home page has two main headlines.

Two competing headlines blur what the page is about, for visitors and for search engines.

Page titles get cut off in Google.

Titles longer than about 60 characters are truncated, so people read half a title and miss the part that names your service.

Two pages share one description.

The home and About pages show the same sentence in results, so the second page has nothing specific to say for itself.

Each finding in a real review comes with the page where I saw it and a plain fix. I don't promise rankings: nobody honest can.
