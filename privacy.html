Privacy

Last updated October 14, 2026.

Permian Local SEO is a one-person local SEO business in Odessa, Texas. This page explains, in plain terms, what happens to your information.

This website

This site has no forms, no accounts, no cookies and no analytics or advertising trackers. The fonts and scripts it uses are served from this same site, so your visit is not shared with third parties by the page itself. Our web host may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and time of visit, for security and reliability.

When you email me

If you write to hello@permianlocalseo.com, I get your email address and whatever you choose to include, such as your business name and website. I use it to reply, to prepare your free review and, if you ask for it, your audit. I don't sell or share it.

If I emailed you first

I sometimes email local business owners in Odessa and Midland using contact details published on their own website or business listing. Each of those emails includes this business's postal address. Reply with the word "unsubscribe" and I will remove you from my list immediately and will not contact you again.

Your audit and your data

Audits are based on public information: your website and your public listings. I may use AI tools to help check them, and I review every note before it is sent. I will not ask for passwords to your accounts.

Contact

Questions, or want your information deleted? Email hello@permianlocalseo.com or write to Permian Local SEO, Odessa, TX.
