Why this hero

Proposal only. Each decision is tied to a rule in docs/reels-hero.md (R1 to R12, synthesis) or in conocimiento/diseno-web/notas.md ("Hero y CTA", "Copy y confianza", "Conversion para pyme local", "Movil").

  1. The headline names the owner's problem and the place.reels-hero R1 (headline centered on the owner's problem, 1-2 lines, dominant size) and notas "headline says what you do and where, in plain words".
  2. One-line subtitle with the concrete result, dimmed.reels-hero R2: 1-2 lines, muted color, result in plain language (more calls, listing fixed).
  3. One amber primary CTA, one outlined secondary, both specific.reels-hero R3 and notas "one main button, secondary ones soft", plus "concrete button text". Buttons are 56 px tall (Fitts), only two options (Hick).
  4. Trust row uses only true facts, no invented stars or logos.reels-hero R4 (proof in the first screen) adapted honestly; notas "free quote" microcopy: "Free, no obligation", "I reply myself", "No sales team".
  5. Asymmetric layout: text left (about 55%), one visual right; stacked on mobile.reels-hero R5 (asymmetric, no generic 3-column grid) and R12 (readable at 375 px, checked in a 375 px frame).
  6. The protagonist is the real product: the 1-page review, built in HTML/CSS.reels-hero R6 and R9 (show the real audit, not stock photos) and notas "a visual protagonist tied to the business story". Findings come from the anonymized sample report.
  7. Same palette: dark ink with one amber accent, Bricolage display type; amber halo behind the sheet.reels-hero R7 (one accent on a neutral base, expressive headline) and reel 2 (black with an orange halo). The pump jack is a 5% texture, not the hero image. Nav stays minimal with one button: R8, notas "familiar patterns" (Jakob).
  8. Small purposeful motion, nothing heavy, all off with reduced motion.reels-hero R10 and R11: words rise in, the sheet slides in and settles, a small floating trust card balances it, the halo breathes over 14 s. No new libraries; text sizes use clamp() (notas "Movil") and hover styles stay inside @media (hover:hover) via the shared .btn.
