Quick answers

Before you call

My breaker keeps tripping. Is it an emergency?

If you smell burning, see sparks or a panel feels hot, switch off the main if it is safe and call us any hour. A breaker that trips now and then can usually wait for a same-week visit.

Do I get a price before you start?

Yes. Estimates are free and the price comes in writing before any work begins. If the scope changes, we ask you first.

Can you install an EV charger at my house?

Yes. We check your panel load, run a dedicated circuit and mount a Level 2 charger for any make of electric vehicle.

Do you pull permits and pass inspection?

When the work needs a permit, we pull it and schedule the inspection. Our goal is clean work that passes the first time.
